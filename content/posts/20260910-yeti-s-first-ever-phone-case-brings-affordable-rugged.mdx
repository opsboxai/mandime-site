Yeti just made a move that nobody expected: a phone case. Not some boutique collaboration or limited drop. An actual, designed-from-scratch protective case for the iPhone 18 and 18 Pro Max, and it costs less than most people think a Yeti product should cost.

The LoadOut case is built the way Yeti builds everything else—obsessive about materials and durability. It's injection-molded with reinforced corners, a raised lip for screen protection, and the kind of grip texture that doesn't get slick when you're actually using it outside. The back is open enough to show off whatever phone color you bought, but the sides are thick enough to take a real hit. For a company that's spent two decades proving it makes gear that survives actual use, not just Instagram posts, this isn't a vanity project.

The real story here isn't the case itself. It's that Yeti saw a gap. Every phone protection company either goes cheap and disposable or charges $70+ for something that looks like you're carrying a briefcase in your pocket. Yeti came in the middle, and that middle ground is exactly where guys who actually use their gear tend to live. The LoadOut works because it doesn't pretend to be armor or a fashion statement. It's just doing its job—protecting a device you can't afford to lose—without apology or bloat.
