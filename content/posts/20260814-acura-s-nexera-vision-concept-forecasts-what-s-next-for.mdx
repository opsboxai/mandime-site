Acura just showed the Nexera Vision, a concept coupe that's basically a look ahead at how the brand plans to retool itself over the next five years. It's a dramatic shift from what's on dealer lots right now, which matters because Acura has spent the last decade getting kind of invisible in the luxury space. The car sits low and wide, with a swept-back roofline and this sharp, minimalist take on the signature Acura grille that actually feels contemporary instead of bolted-on. Road & Track notes that design cues from this thing will show up on the next generation of production cars, which is the whole point of a vision concept.

What makes this interesting isn't the futuristic styling alone. It's that Honda's luxury division is clearly betting on a completely different visual language to stand out from German competition and the wave of new EV-native brands. The Nexera leans hard into simplicity and proportion instead of aggressive creases and overwrought details. If Acura actually executes on that restraint when these cars hit the road, they could look genuinely different in a way that doesn't scream "we hired a new design boss." The real test is whether Honda will actually greenlight the bold stuff or water it down until it reaches production, which is where most concepts die anyway.
