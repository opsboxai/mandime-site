Ram sold out every 5.7 Hemi Rumble Bee they can build until March 2027 in less than a day. That's not a flex from the marketing team. That's actual humans lining up to drop $60K-plus on a truck with a naturally aspirated V8 in an era when everyone's supposed to be buying electric or turbo fours.

The Rumble Bee nameplate is back after a 15-year gap, and they brought the actual engine people wanted instead of some hybrid compromise or displacement-deleted nonsense. Full 5.7L Hemi, 390 horsepower, the kind of power you feel in your chest at a stoplight. Yellow and black special edition paint. It's not subtle, and apparently the market hates subtlety when it comes to what you drive.

This matters because Detroit keeps getting told that V8s are dying, that truck buyers are aging out, that the future is all efficiency and software. And maybe all that's true for the overall market. But there's clearly a ceiling on how much people will compromise on what they actually want. You can build electric trucks and dual-turbo trucks and plug-in hybrids, but when someone puts the old V8 nameplate back on the menu with the engine actually in it, the allocation sells in hours. That's worth paying attention to.
