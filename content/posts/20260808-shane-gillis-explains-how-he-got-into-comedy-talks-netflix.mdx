Shane Gillis isn't your average comedian. He's the guy who broke the Guinness World Record for the most tickets sold for a solo comedy show. That's right, he sold more tickets than anyone else in history. But how did he get there? In his chat with Jimmy Fallon on The Tonight Show, Gillis opens up about his journey from stand-up newbie to record-breaking star. And if that wasn't enough, he also talks about his new Netflix show, Tires, filmed in a real tire shop in Pennsylvania. If you're curious about the life of a comedy trailblazer, this interview is your front-row seat.
