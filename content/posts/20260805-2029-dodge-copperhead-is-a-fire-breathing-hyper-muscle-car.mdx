Dodge just committed to building a 2029 hyper-muscle car called the Copperhead, and the rendering shows something that actually looks like it could exist instead of the usual vaporware fantasy that gets passed around online. This isn't some designer's fever dream posted in a forum. Car and Driver sourced this, which means there's real meat on the bone here.

The timing matters. Dodge has been running out the clock on gas engines while the industry goes electric, so they're using these last few years to build something genuinely unhinged before the regulatory hammer drops. A fire-breathing hyper-muscle car in 2029 means they're planning to cram as much raw performance into an ICE platform as they possibly can before the window closes. That's the whole point. This isn't a compromise vehicle or a toned-down concept. It's the last hurrah, and Dodge knows it.

What makes this interesting isn't just the specs (which we don't have yet) but the philosophy behind it. Dodge is saying loud and clear that they're not going quietly into the EV transition like some manufacturers. They're spending these final years of freedom building something audacious enough that people will still be talking about it after it's gone. Whether the Copperhead actually hits the market or becomes another dead concept, the fact that Dodge is greenlighting this kind of project tells you everything about where their head's at and why the muscle car loyalists are still paying attention.
