Project Farm just put Walmart batteries to the test, and the results are about as exciting as a dead battery in the middle of winter. He bought 4 of the same style of batteries from Walmart and 5 from O’Reilly Auto Parts, and the Walmart ones didn’t exactly shine. From cold cranking amps to reserve capacity, these batteries fell flat. If you’re looking for reliable power, you might want to skip the Walmart aisle and head straight to O’Reilly.
