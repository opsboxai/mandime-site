Fat Bear Week is back September 22-29, and Outside Online set up a personality quiz to match you with one of the contenders. It's the kind of thing that sounds ridiculous until you realize the bears themselves are genuinely fascinating, each with a different strategy for surviving the brutal Alaska winter.

The premise is simple: bears pack on weight all summer and fall, and Fat Bear Week celebrates which ones do it best. But it's not just about raw tonnage. Some bears are aggressive hunters, some are scavengers, some work the salmon runs harder than others. Your bear type probably says something about how you approach your own survival. The quiz picks up on that. Are you the type who goes all-in on one strategy, or do you play the opportunist game, snacking wherever calories are available?

It's not deep psychology. But it's a clever hook into something legitimately wild about nature: these bears are in an arms race against starvation. The ones who pack on the most fat in a few months live. The ones who don't, don't. And yeah, there's something weirdly compelling about watching it happen year after year and rooting for your bear. Hit the quiz and find out which one you actually are.
