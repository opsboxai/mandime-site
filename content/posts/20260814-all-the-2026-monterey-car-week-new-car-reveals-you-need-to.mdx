Monterey Car Week just became the place to be if you actually care about what's coming next. Road & Track rounded up every major reveal hitting the peninsula in 2026, and the lineup is dense enough to justify clearing your calendar for the whole week. This isn't some corporate press conference circuit. These are the cars manufacturers want to show off to the people who understand them, which means the stuff landing here tends to be either genuinely significant or genuinely weird, sometimes both.

The event pulls in everything from hypercar debuts to electrified versions of icons that probably didn't need electrifying, all crammed into a week of auctions, track events, and informal reveals scattered across Pebble Beach and Big Sur. It's chaos in the best way. Unlike CES or Geneva Motor Show, there's no rigid schedule or assigned booth, which means you'll stumble onto announcements and first looks in parking lots and hillside galleries. That unpredictability is half the appeal. The other half is that if a manufacturer thinks their car is cool enough, they'll bring it here instead of waiting for a trade show. Which tells you everything you need to know about what's actually worth paying attention to.
