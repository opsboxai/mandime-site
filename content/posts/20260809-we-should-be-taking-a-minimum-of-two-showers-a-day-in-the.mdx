Sweat isn't just uncomfortable, it's a hygiene problem that one shower can't solve in peak summer heat. You're wearing the same clothes, sitting in AC then walking into 95-degree humidity, sweating through your sheets at night, and wondering why you feel grimy by 2 p.m. One shower handles maybe half that equation.

The math is simple: morning shower gets you clean for the day. Evening shower actually matters now because you've accumulated sweat, deodorant residue, bacteria from sitting around, and whatever humidity decided to stick to your skin. Your bed stays cleaner. You sleep better. Your sheets don't turn into a petri dish by Friday. That's not neurotic, that's maintenance.

The trick is keeping them short. Five minutes, not twenty. Cold water helps, especially the second one. You're not trying to waste water or time, just reset your body twice when it actually needs it. Swap out wet clothes immediately. Skip the heavy products the second time around. Keep a second set of sheets ready so rotation isn't a chore. This isn't about being precious about cleanliness. It's about not feeling disgusting and actually enjoying summer instead of just surviving it until October.
