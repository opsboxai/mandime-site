Leon Hedgepeth pulled seven celebrity fits from the month's biggest stages—World Cup boxes, Wimbledon finals, fashion week front rows—and actually broke down how to build them yourself instead of just showing you unattainable rich-guy fantasy wear. That's the move. Most of these listicles just post a photo of some actor in a Tom Ford suit that costs four grand and call it inspiration. Hedgepeth went deeper, flagging the real details that matter: fabric weights, color combos that actually work together, where the proportions land. The stuff that translates when you're shopping at normal retailers insteadessing from a luxury stylist's unlimited budget.

The month's biggest cultural moments tend to surface the same five outfits across every men's site. Hedgepeth caught the ones that didn't make the rounds yet, which means there's actual discovery here. Summer dressing is predictable by design, but the gap between boring and sharp usually comes down to one or two specific choices. A collar treatment, a weight of fabric, the way a shirt fits through the shoulders. Those are the details that separate a fit that lands from one that looks like you grabbed whatever was clean.
