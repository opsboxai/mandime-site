A hiking couple in Banff National Park just learned that going viral can cost you twenty-five grand. They posted footage of a bear encounter that blew up online, but park officials were watching too. The real problem: their dog was off-leash when they came face-to-face with the bear, which is explicitly prohibited in the park.

The video looked dramatic enough to rack up views, but it exposed a clear violation of park rules designed specifically to protect both people and wildlife. An unleashed dog near a bear isn't a cute moment. It's a threat to the animal, a liability to the hikers, and exactly the kind of reckless behavior that forces parks to implement stricter regulations. The fine reflects how seriously Banff takes these rules, and rightfully so. One loose dog panicking or aggressing toward a bear can escalate a manageable situation into something genuinely dangerous.

The irony is thick: their video was probably meant to showcase the raw beauty of hiking in Banff, but instead it's becoming a cautionary tale about why the rules exist. Most people follow leash laws. These two just happened to film their violation and share it with hundreds of thousands of people, which is essentially the same as sending the park wardens a written confession with timestamps.
