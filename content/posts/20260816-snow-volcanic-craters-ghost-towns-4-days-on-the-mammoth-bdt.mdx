OTG is taking you on a wild 4-day adventure through the Mammoth Backcountry Discovery Trail (BDT). Imagine 360 miles of volcanic terrain, alpine passes, and ghost towns, all wrapped up in the unpredictable Eastern Sierra. You'll weather surprise snowstorms, navigate through volcanic craters and obsidian domes, and even soak in the geothermal hot springs of Hot Creek. And if that's not enough, they're pushing up into the Sweetwater Range to tackle one of the highest drivable roads in California. This isn't just a trip; it's an expedition into the heart of the Sierra's untamed beauty. Hit play to see it all unfold.
