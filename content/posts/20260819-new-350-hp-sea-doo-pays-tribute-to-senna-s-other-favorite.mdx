Ayrton Senna owned jet skis the way most people own cars. The three-time F1 champion wasn't just collecting toys—he was genuinely into them, which is the kind of detail that gets buried until someone builds a tribute machine around it. Sea-Doo just did exactly that: a 350-horsepower jet ski dressed in Senna's iconic yellow and green helmet livery, complete with his trademark S motif splashed across the hull. It hits 60 in about three seconds, which tracks perfectly with a guy who made his living converting lap time into art.

What makes this land harder than a straight licensing cash-grab is the specificity of it. They didn't slap his colors on a random model—they built something fast enough to actually deserve the name. That 350 horses means this thing moves like something Senna would've actually wanted to ride, not something gathering dust in a collector's garage. The yellow and green aren't some marketing afterthought either. Those are the colors that defined him: the helmet you see in every famous photo, the one that made him instantly recognizable at 200 mph. On water, they hit different.

For anyone who thought Senna was only a race driver, this is a solid reminder that the obsession ran deeper. Some people have hobbies. Champions have them too, they just happen faster and look better doing it.
