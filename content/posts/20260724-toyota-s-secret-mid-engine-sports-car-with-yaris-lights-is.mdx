Toyota is actually building the MR2 again, and they're not even trying to hide it anymore. A mid-engine sports car with Yaris headlights spotted testing at Fuji Speedway is the clearest signal yet that the company's genuinely committed to reviving one of the most beloved affordable roadsters ever made. The fact that they're using existing Yaris lighting suggests this isn't some far-off concept, but something they're actively engineering to production specs.

This matters because the MR2 has been dead since 2007, and nothing Toyota's released in that gap has scratched the same itch. Mid-engine cars are rare at any price, rarer still below six figures. If they actually deliver a lightweight, mid-mounted, affordable driver's car in 2025 or 2026, they're not just bringing back a nameplate, they're filling a void that practically no one else is touching. The automotive world doesn't need another crossover or sedan. It needs this.
