On August 12, 2026, Heart Aerospace's X1 took to the skies, becoming the world's largest electric aircraft ever flown. With a 106-foot wingspan and a takeoff weight over 25,000 pounds, this beast of a plane completed a 27-minute flight, including 8 minutes in the air. The kicker? It used just $5 worth of electricity.
