If you thought your LG Smart TV was just a screen for your shows, think again. Gamers Nexus dug into this one and found that your TV might be watching you more than you're watching it. LG's Smart TVs come with ACR (automatic content recognition) that can track your viewing habits with alarming accuracy. And get this: LG Ad Solutions' execs are on record saying they "own the glass," not you. That means they know who's in your house, what you're watching, and even your secondary devices.
