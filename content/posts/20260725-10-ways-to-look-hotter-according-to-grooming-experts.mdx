Most grooming advice lands somewhere between obvious and useless. But the difference between looking good and looking like you actually tried is smaller than you think, and it usually comes down to the stuff nobody talks about.

Adam Hurly tracked down actual grooming professionals—not Instagram influencers or sponsored brand reps—and the takeaway is that the shortcuts don't work. What does work is consistency with the basics: a real skincare routine that matches your skin type, not the one your barber's cousin recommended. Getting a proper haircut from someone who understands your face shape and hair texture, then maintaining it. Dressing in clothes that actually fit your body instead of whatever was on sale. The unglamorous reality is that 80 percent of looking better is just showing up clean, groomed, and dressed intentionally. The other 20 percent is posture and confidence, which sounds cliché until you realize most guys skip both entirely.

The expert consensus also reveals something useful about the stuff guys stress over: expensive skincare products matter way less than sun protection and moisturizing consistently. A solid beard takes weeks to fill in properly before you decide if you want to keep it. Teeth get whiter from actually brushing and flossing, not teeth-whitening strips. None of this is complicated or expensive. It's just work that compounds quietly until one day you catch yourself in a mirror and realize you look different. Better. That's the point.
