Zinc isn't one of those supplements that makes big promises and delivers nothing. Your body actually needs it to do basic stuff like fight off colds, heal wounds, and keep your skin from looking like a problem that needs solving. Most guys don't think about it until they're deficient, which is stupid, because the difference between enough and not enough is noticeable.

Here's what actually matters. Zinc shores up your immune system, which means fewer weeks getting wrecked by whatever's going around the office. It's involved in wound healing at a cellular level, so if you get cut or scraped up, your body can't repair it properly without adequate zinc. Your skin clears up when you have enough of it, because zinc regulates oil production and has legit anti-inflammatory properties. Your vision stays sharper longer, because the mineral concentrates in your eye tissue and protects against age-related decline. And it bolsters testosterone production, which is not a selling point you see in mainstream articles but which every guy reading this suddenly cares about.

The catch is you can't just megadose it. Too much zinc tanks your copper absorption and creates its own problems. You're looking at 11 milligrams a day for adult men, which you can get from oysters, beef, pumpkin seeds, or a straightforward supplement if your diet is thin on those sources. Most guys eating reasonably are probably fine, but if you're dealing with persistent infections, slow healing, or skin that won't cooperate, a blood test might show you're running low. That's worth knowing.
