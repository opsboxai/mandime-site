Here's the truth: if you're eating chicken breast three times a week because you think it's the only way to hit your protein targets, you're making meal prep harder than it needs to be. Emily Laurence Sardinha put together seven high-protein dishes that actually taste like food, which matters way more than the macro math on paper.

The range here is what makes it work. You've got quinoa bowls, which sit at around 8 grams of protein per cooked cup and pair with basically anything you have lying around. Then there's tofu, the move most guys skip because they assume it'll taste like nothing. Done right, it doesn't. The piece covers easy preparations that don't require a culinary degree or a two-hour cook time. There's also beef that isn't just a steak, fish options beyond salmon, and legume-based dishes that won't leave you feeling like you're eating cardboard for gains.

The real angle here is that protein variety keeps you sane. Your taste buds adapt to repetition fast, which is why so many people quit structured eating after three weeks. Rotating between five or six solid protein sources means you're actually staying consistent instead of white-knuckling through chicken number forty-two. Hit your numbers, eat something different, move on with your day. That's the whole thing.
