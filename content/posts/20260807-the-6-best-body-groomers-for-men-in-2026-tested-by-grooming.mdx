Body groomers have gotten genuinely useful in the last few years. Not the clumsy multitools from a decade ago, but actual purpose-built trimmers that handle body hair without the friction, irritation, or 20-minute setup time of traditional razors. The difference matters more than it sounds, especially if you're dealing with thicker hair or sensitive skin anywhere below the neck.

The real divide in this category is between wet-dry models and dry-only ones. Wet-dry groomers let you work in the shower with soap or shaving cream, which cuts down on razor burn and ingrown hairs significantly. You're also not fumbling around your chest or back with a dry blade and no glide. That alone makes it worth the extra money for most guys. Dry-only trimmers are faster and more portable, but they're less forgiving if your skin is reactive or if you're working on areas you can't see directly.

Guard length matters more than the brand name. Most guys think they want the shortest guard setting available and then regret it immediately. Start one setting longer than you think you need. You can always go shorter next time. Battery life on the better models now hits 90 minutes or more on a full charge, which means you're not waiting around for a quick top-up before you use it. Blade quality degrades fast if you skip cleaning and oiling after each use. That's the maintenance cost nobody talks about but everyone discovers the hard way.
