A few weeks ago, Ghost Town Living stumbled upon a small crack in the ground while hiking around Cerro Gordo. What started as a casual observation turned into a deep dive into history, with six separate trips underground and hundreds of feet of rope. This isn't just another abandoned mine; it's a time capsule, with artifacts like carbide lamps, dynamite boxes, and newspapers from 1897 and 1926. If you're into history and adventure, this one's for you.
