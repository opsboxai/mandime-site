There are now ten Spider-Man movies in theaters or streaming, and ranking them is either the easiest or hardest thing you could do depending on how much time you've actually spent watching them all. Some are genuinely good. Some are the cinematic equivalent of a superhero landing on your face. And then there's Morbius, which exists in a category of confusion all its own.

The Tobey Maguire trilogy set the bar. Spider-Man 2 still holds up as a legitimately excellent action film with real stakes and a villain who isn't just a guy in a suit yelling exposition. The Tom Holland films found their footing once they stopped trying to be Iron Man movies and let him actually be a teenager. Andrew Garfield was phenomenal in a pair of films that got weighed down by studio interference and too many villains crammed into the second one.

The most recent entries have split time between universes, alternate dimensions, and apparently whatever the hell Sony was thinking when they made Madame Web. That's where the list gets interesting. Not all of these movies are worth rewatching. Some are worth watching at all. But if you're going to sit through ten Spider-Man films to figure out which ones actually matter, you might as well know which five you can skip without missing anything except wasted time.
