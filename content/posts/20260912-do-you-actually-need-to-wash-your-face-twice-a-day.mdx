Most guys wash their face on autopilot. Morning shower, nighttime before bed, done. But dermatologists are pretty clear on this one: twice a day is overkill for most men, and it might actually be working against you.

Your skin produces oil and builds up dead cells naturally. That's not a problem that needs solving twice. Over-washing strips your skin barrier, which triggers your face to compensate by producing even more oil. You end up in a cycle where you're washing more because your skin feels greasier, when really you caused the grease by washing too much. If you've got dry skin or live somewhere cold, you're just accelerating flaking and irritation for no reason.

The real answer depends on what you're actually doing. If you're hitting the gym or working outside, a rinse after that makes sense. If you're sitting in an office all day, morning and night is probably unnecessary. Most dermatologists say a single good wash at night when you've actually accumulated dirt, sweat, and dead skin is the baseline. Morning can just be a quick rinse with water if your skin doesn't feel grimy. Oily skin types might justify twice a day, but even then, one of those should be water-only or a gentle splash.

The other variable nobody talks about: what you're using matters more than how often. A harsh cleanser twice a day will wreck your skin faster than a good one once a day. If you're using actual face wash, you probably don't need to do it as much as you think. Experiment for a week or two by dropping to once daily at night and see if your skin actually improves. Most guys find it does.
